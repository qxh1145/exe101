# Product Brief: Rescom

## Executive Summary

University students depend on primary survey data for their scientific research and graduation theses. However, the traditional method of collecting this data—spamming links in Facebook and Zalo groups—has collapsed. It yields low response rates, heavily biased samples, and "garbage" data that ultimately degrades the quality of their scientific reports. 

Rescom is a centralized survey exchange ecosystem (similar to SurveySwap) that solves this by introducing a technology-enforced Point System. By creating a fair, guaranteed exchange of effort, Rescom ensures students can reach their exact target demographics and collect high-quality, verified data without the stress of unmet deadlines or compromised academic integrity.

## The Problem

For university students (particularly in Business, Marketing, and Communications), primary data collection is a mandatory and high-stakes requirement for scientific assessments. Without good data, they cannot produce a defensible scientific report, directly impacting their academic standing. 

Currently, students face five compounding failures in the status quo:
1. **Inefficiency (Signal-to-Noise):** Traditional survey spamming is universally ignored. Links are quickly buried in crowded social media groups.
2. **Sample Bias:** Students cannot filter respondents. A survey needing "3rd-year Marketing students" gets answered indiscriminately by anyone willing to click.
3. **The "Free Rider" Problem:** The current culture of *xin xỏ chéo* (cross-begging) relies on goodwill, meaning many students take without giving back, creating resentment and inequality.
4. **Data Degradation (Spam):** Desperate students often resort to fake or rushed responses just to hit their sample size quotas, ruining the dataset's validity.
5. **Deadline Anxiety:** The manual grind of begging for responses creates immense pressure as research deadlines approach.

## The Solution

Rescom solves this through a closed-loop **Survey Exchange Ecosystem**. Instead of relying on goodwill, it operates on a strict, transactional economy powered by a "Point System." 

The core experience is a 5-step loop:
1. **Publish:** A student posts their survey and assigns a point bounty.
2. **Target Matching:** The student strictly defines the demographic criteria required (e.g., specific major, age, or university).
3. **Execution:** The community answers the survey.
4. **Verification:** The system automatically verifies completion (via unique codes or internal form builders) and checks for anti-fraud signals (like completing a survey too quickly).
5. **Transaction:** Points are instantly and securely transferred from the publisher to the respondent via ACID database transactions.

## What Makes This Different

Rescom’s unfair advantage is that it shifts the paradigm from charity to a **win-win economy with real monetary value**. 

While inspired by models like SurveySwap, Rescom goes further:
* **Real Monetary Value:** The Point Economy is real. Ultimately, users can withdraw points into real cash, converting academic data collection into a legitimate micro-task economy in Vietnam.
* **Guaranteed Exchange:** The system locks points in escrow and only releases them upon verified completion, eliminating the "free rider" problem.
* **Technological Guardrails:** Anti-fraud mechanics (time-barriers, attention-checks) ensure that the data being paid for is actually high-quality, protecting the integrity of the ecosystem.

## Who This Serves

- **Primary Users (The Beachhead):** Students at FPT University Da Nang (specifically in Business, Marketing, Media, and Languages) who have mandatory research courses and graduation theses requiring primary data.
- **Secondary Users (Future Scope):** University faculty, scientific research departments, and eventually students across other universities in Vietnam.

## Success Criteria

*[ASSUMPTION]* To prove the SurveySwap model works in this specific market with the real-money twist, Phase 1 success looks like:
- **Marketplace Liquidity:** 80% of published surveys reach their target response quota within 72 hours.
- **Network Growth:** 500 active users within the first month at the beachhead campus.
- **Data Integrity:** Less than 5% of responses rejected by the anti-fraud time barrier.

## Scope

**In for V1 (Phase 1):**
- Core point economy engine and 5-step exchange loop.
- Target matching engine (demographic filtering).
- External form verification (Google/Microsoft Forms via completion codes).
- Locked Starter Points & Real-time time barrier anti-fraud systems.

**Explicitly Out of V1:**
- Internal Form Builder (Save for Phase 2).
- Real money withdrawal/fiat wallet integration (V1 establishes point liquidity first).
- Advanced B2B researcher dashboards.

## Vision

To become the de facto verified data collection network for academic research in Vietnam, expanding from a student-to-student peer economy into a trusted B2B platform for universities and commercial market researchers.
